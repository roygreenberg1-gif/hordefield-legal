ZomBlitz support

ZomBlitz: Gun Heroes · published by Roy Greenberg

Contact: email playrg.games@gmail.com with a short description of the problem, your device model (for example "Galaxy A06" or "iPhone 14 Pro") and the game version (shown at the bottom of the game's own Settings; on Android also in the phone's Settings > Apps > ZomBlitz). The CONTACT US button in the game's Settings fills these in for you. We answer in English, usually within two working days.

Your progress

  • Your progress is saved on your device. On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games (CLOUD SAVE in the game's Settings), a copy is backed up to that account, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Without it, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress. On iPhone the game makes no backup.

TEAM and Quick Match

  • In Quick Match, computer-controlled teammates may fill empty team seats. In TEAM and Quick Match, a computer-controlled teammate may carry on the hero of a player who leaves the run, loses connection or is away. Computer-controlled teammates may also give a LIKE on the results screen. After an online Quick Match, the players who choose PLAY AGAIN TOGETHER stay as a team, and other players may then join the empty seats.

Found a bug?

  • Email us what happened, what you were doing just before, and a screenshot if you can.

Your data

  • The game sends game statistics and crash reports as described in the Privacy Policy. It has no account of its own; on Android it offers optional reward videos and in-game purchases through Google Play.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about your installation, use CONTACT US in the game's Settings (or DELETE DATA, to delete statistics); both fill in your Player ID. We answer within 30 days, and we complete a deletion within 30 days.
  • The rules for playing are in the Terms of Service.
