ZomBlitz support

ZomBlitz: Gun Heroes · published by Roy Greenberg

Contact: email playrg.games@gmail.com with a short description of the problem, your device model (for example "Galaxy A06" or "iPhone 14 Pro") and the game version (shown at the bottom of the game's own Settings; on Android also in the phone's Settings > Apps > ZomBlitz). The CONTACT US button in the game's Settings fills these in for you. We answer in English, usually within two working days.

Your progress

  • Your progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, deletes it, and it does not move to a new phone.

Found a bug?

  • Email us what happened, what you were doing just before, and a screenshot if you can.

Your data

  • The game sends game statistics and crash reports as described in the Privacy Policy; it has no account and no ads.
  • The rules for playing are in the Terms of Service.
