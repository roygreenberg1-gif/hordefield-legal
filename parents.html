ZomBlitz: a page for parents

Last updated: 13 November 2026

ZomBlitz: Gun Heroes is a cartoon zombie shooter made for a general audience of teens and adults, not directed at children under 13; players under 18 may play only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian. This page explains, in plain words, what a young player meets in the game and what you can control.

What is in the game

  • Cartoon fights against zombies, with guns and explosions. Blood and strong flashes can be turned off in SETTINGS > EFFECTS.
  • No free chat: there is no way to send your own text, pictures or voice to other players. In team runs, players can send only a fixed set of short phrases built into the game (such as HELP! or NICE!), place markers on the map, and give each other a like.
  • TEAM mode lets up to four friends play together. A friend joins only with a 4-letter room code that a player chooses to share. In a TEAM room, the other players see a player's Google Play Games name (Android) or Game Center name (iPhone) if the player is signed in to it; otherwise a gamer tag the game makes. Game traffic between the players is not encrypted. A player under 13 (by the age screen or the store) always gets a made-up name, a new one for every match. If a player leaves a run, loses connection or is away, a computer-controlled teammate may carry on their hero.
  • QUICK MATCH puts a player in a team without a code, with other players who may be strangers. There, everyone is shown only by a name the game makes up, never by an account name, and computer-controlled teammates may fill empty seats.
  • Weekly league tables are against computer rivals; no other player's results are shown there.
  • A player under 13 (by the age screen or the store) can still play TEAM and QUICK MATCH: only with the fixed phrases, markers and likes, under a name the game makes up (a new one for every match), and possibly with computer-controlled teammates.

Limiting play

The game has no switch of its own that turns off TEAM and QUICK MATCH. On Android, Google Family Link lets you block the game completely, set a daily time limit for it, and require your approval before apps are downloaded or purchases are made; it has no setting that blocks only the game's online play. On iPhone, Screen Time lets you set an app limit or block the app.

Purchases and videos

  • The game is free. Where real-money purchases are offered, they go through Google Play or the App Store, and the store asks for its own confirmation.
  • Nothing random is sold for money: chests and wheels in the game are earned by playing.
  • You can require your approval for every purchase: on Android with Google Family Link or "Require authentication for purchases" in Google Play; on iPhone with Ask to Buy (Family Sharing) or Screen Time.
  • If the store tells the game that a player is a minor whose parent has not approved purchases, the game blocks purchases.
  • If the store, or the player's answer on the age screen, says the player is under 13: the game sends no statistics; on Android it shows only reward videos that are non-personalised and suitable for children. On both, it sets no reminders, offers no INVITE FRIENDS and does not ask for a store rating.

Information the game handles

ZomBlitz asks a player's age once and keeps it only on the device. Players under 13 get no statistics and no friend invites, and on Android only child-appropriate, non-personalised reward videos, and players under 18 get no advertising measurement.

The game asks no name, email, phone number or birthday. What it sends, and the choices a player has, are in the Privacy Policy. On iPhone, answering NO THANKS to the statistics question sends nothing at all.

Asking us to delete information, or anything else

Open SETTINGS in the game and tap CONTACT US, which writes an email with the Player ID for you; or note the Player ID shown at the bottom of SETTINGS and write to playrg.games@gmail.com with it. A parent can ask us this way to delete what we hold about a child's installation. We answer parents' requests first, and we complete a deletion within 30 days. Refunds of store purchases are made by Google Play or Apple, under their own refund policies.
