What the invite does

Your friend shared their six-character code with you. If you enter it in your first week, before you have won stage 10, you join their game. When you win stage 5 you get a welcome gift in the game, and as you play on, your friend may get in-game rewards too. Rewards come from playing, never from installing. A code can be entered once, and never your own.

What data is involved

  • This page stores nothing, sets no cookies and sends nothing of its own. On Android it passes the code on to Google Play inside the store link; on iPhone it copies the code when you tap the button. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives the address asked for, code included, and your IP address.
  • In the game, joining adds a random identifier of an anonymous account the game creates for you (no name, email or password) to your friend's code's record; winning stages 10 and 40 adds it once more each. Anyone who knows the code can read that code's record.
  • For a player under 13 (by the age screen or the store), invites do not exist at all.

The details are in the Privacy Policy.
