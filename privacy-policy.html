Privacy Policy for Hordefield

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Hordefield: Zombie Survival ("Hordefield", "the game") is developed and published by Roy Greenberg. This policy applies to the Android application with the package name com.greenberg.hordefield and to the iPhone application with the bundle identifier com.greenberg.hordefield.

The short version

The game has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and does not sell or share personal information. It sends game statistics and crash reports to Google's Firebase service so we can find what is hard and fix what breaks: on Android, basic game counts and crash reports always, and detailed statistics only if you agree; on iPhone, nothing unless you agree. Your progress stays on your device.

What the game does not collect

  • No name, phone number or account of any kind (an email address only if you write to us).
  • No precise location. Firebase works out a rough area (country or city) from the connection's IP address; the address itself is masked and not kept.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • Advertising ID: the game does not collect it. Firebase's collection of it is switched off and the game shows no ads, although the Android permission for it is present.

Game statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google, for us. Nothing is sent before you answer the statistics question that appears when you first start the game.

  • What is sent: game events such as a stage started or ended and how it went, coins and diamonds earned and spent, whether a reward video or a purchase went through; crash reports (what the game was doing when it stopped, the phone model and system version); general facts sent with every event (phone model, system version, language, game version, when the game was first opened, and how many times it has been played); and an installation identifier that Firebase creates for this copy of the game.
  • On Android: basic game counts and crash reports are always sent. If you choose "YES, SHARE MORE" (or switch on DETAILED STATISTICS in Settings), detailed statistics are also kept for this installation, together with your Player ID, so a deletion request can find them. The same Player ID appears in emails you send us from the game.
  • On iPhone: nothing is sent unless you answer "YES". If you answer "NO, THANKS", or switch STATISTICS off in Settings, no statistics and no crash reports are sent.
  • How long: Analytics data about your installation is kept for 14 months; crash reports for 90 days. Totals and summary reports that do not identify any player or installation may be kept longer.

You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings.

Writing to us, and test builds

CONTACT US in Settings opens your own mail app with a message that includes the game's version, your phone's model and system, and your Player ID (a random code made by the game, not tied to your phone or any account). We receive it only if you send it, together with your email address. Support emails are deleted when the issue is closed, or on request. Test builds can also share a play log of your attempts, only when you press SEND TEST LOG and choose where to send it. Test builds also show TEST reward videos and TEST purchases: these are screens made by us, cost nothing, and contact no advertising network or payment service.

On iPhone

To stop timed rewards from being collected early by changing the phone's clock, the iPhone version asks Apple's web server (www.apple.com) for the current time. The request carries nothing about you or your game. As with any internet request, Apple's server sees your device's IP address; Apple's own privacy policy covers that. The game does not record or keep it.

Where your progress is stored

Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device, protected against editing. They are not backed up to any server by the game. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

The app stores

Google Play and the App Store handle the download and may collect information about it under their own privacy policies. We receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics the stores give every developer (for example, how many people installed the game).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families programme. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete the statistics kept for your installation: write to the address below with the Player ID shown in the game's Settings. Basic counts (Android's BASIC ONLY) carry no Player ID, so they cannot be singled out for deletion; they are removed with the retention periods above. If you have any other question about your privacy, write to us too.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it does with information, for example by adding purchases, rewarded videos or statistics, this policy is updated before that version is released, and the date above changes.
