Privacy Policy for ZomBlitz

Last updated: 13 November 2026

ZomBlitz: Gun Heroes ("ZomBlitz", "the game") is developed and published by Roy Greenberg. This policy applies to the Android application with the package name com.greenberg.hordefield and to the iPhone application with the bundle identifier com.greenberg.hordefield.

The short version

The game has no account of its own and does not sell personal information. If you are 18 or over and agree to statistics outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, they are also used to measure our advertising (below). On Android you may sign in to Google Play Games, but you do not have to. It sends game statistics and crash reports to Google's Firebase service so we can find what is hard and fix what breaks: on Android, an anonymous "app opened" count from the first start (outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), basic game counts and crash reports once you answer the statistics question, and detailed statistics only if you agree (once the game knows a player is under 13, no statistics at all; see Children); on iPhone, nothing unless you agree. Your progress stays on your device, and on Android it is also backed up to your Google Play Games account if you sign in to it. TEAM mode and Quick Match connect you with other players through the Photon multiplayer service. On Android, the game offers optional reward videos from Google AdMob, always non-personalised, and sells in-game items through Google Play; our server checks each purchase with Google Play before it is delivered. The game gives each installation a random, anonymous sign-in with Google's Firebase service, used to check purchases and for INVITE FRIENDS.

Roy Greenberg, an individual developer in Israel, decides how this information is used (he is its "controller" under Israel's Privacy Protection Law); you can reach him at the address at the end of this policy. You are not required by law to give the game any information. If you answer NO THANKS, or switch statistics off later, the game works the same; only what this policy says is always sent is sent (on iPhone, no statistics at all). TEAM mode, Quick Match, purchases and INVITE FRIENDS need the information described in their sections in order to work, so if you do not want that information used, do not use those features.

What the game does not collect

  • No phone number, and we do not ask for your name (an email address only if you write to us). One exception: if you are signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, its display name, which can be your real name, is sent through Photon to the other players in a TEAM room (joined with a room code), and only to them. Quick Match uses a gamer tag the game makes for you (see "Team mode and Quick Match").
  • No payment details: Google Play handles payment, and we never receive your card, your name or your email address from it.
  • No precise location. Firebase works out a rough area (country or city) from the connection's IP address; the address itself is masked and not kept.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, if you answer "YES, COUNT ME IN" (or switch on DETAILED STATISTICS) and you are outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and you are 18 or over (see "Your age"), Firebase reads it so that Google Ads can tell us which of our advertisements brought you to the game and whether you went on to play (for example, won stage 1, 5 or 10). It is not used to choose the advertisements you see. Otherwise Firebase does not read it. Google AdMob, which serves the reward videos, may read it on your phone when a video is requested, as described below, except for a player under 13, whose video requests are marked as child-directed, so Google does not use it (see Children). You can reset or delete it in your phone's settings.

Game statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google, for us. On Android, outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, from the first start and before you answer the statistics question, the game sends one anonymous "app opened" event and Firebase's own automatic counts (such as first open and session start), without your Player ID or the advertising ID, and with analytics storage denied; none of the game's other events and no crash reports are sent until you answer. Elsewhere, and on iPhone, nothing is sent before you answer.

  • What is sent: game events such as a stage started or ended and how it went, coins and diamonds earned and spent and what they were spent on, heroes, weapons and other items received, and on Android whether a reward video or a purchase went through (for a purchase, which item and its price; for a video, the amount Google AdMob reports it earned us); crash reports (what the game was doing when it stopped, the phone model and system version); general facts sent with every event (phone model, system version, language, game version, when the game was first opened, and how many times it has been played); and an installation identifier that Firebase creates for this copy of the game. On iPhone, Firebase may also read the identifier for vendor (IDFV), a code Apple gives each developer for its own apps on a device; it is not the advertising identifier.
  • On Android: basic game counts and crash reports are always sent once you answer. If you choose "YES, COUNT ME IN" (or switch on DETAILED STATISTICS in Settings), detailed statistics are also kept for this installation, together with your Player ID, so a deletion request can find them. The same Player ID appears in emails you send us from the game. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a yes from a player aged 18 or over also lets Google Ads measure our advertising campaigns with these statistics (consent for ad storage and ad user data; never for personalised advertising).
  • On iPhone: nothing is sent unless you answer "YES, COUNT ME IN". If you answer "NO THANKS", or switch STATISTICS off in Settings, no statistics and no crash reports are sent.
  • Settings from our side (Remote Config): the game also uses Firebase Remote Config, from Google, so we can change some of its settings without an update (for example, switch a feature off if it breaks) and try variants of the game with groups of players. It starts at the same time as the statistics above (on iPhone only if you answer "YES, COUNT ME IN"), uses the same installation identifier, and sends nothing else about you.
  • How long: Analytics data about your installation is kept for 14 months; crash reports for 90 days. Totals and summary reports that do not identify any player or installation may be kept longer.

You can change your answer at any time in the game's Settings.

Google processes this data only to provide these services to us, under terms that give it the same or equal protection as this policy.

Reward videos (Google AdMob, Android)

On Android, the game offers optional reward videos (for example, a revive or extra coins). A video is requested only for an offer on screen, and plays only when you tap to watch it. The videos are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. Every request asks for non-personalised advertising: the advertisements are not chosen from your interests or your activity in other apps, and the content is limited to a PG rating (G for a player under 13; see below). To serve a video, count it, limit how often the same one is shown and prevent fraud, AdMob receives from your phone your device's IP address, the phone's model, system and language, the game's identity, how you interact with the advertisement and, on Android, the advertising ID (which Google does not use for a player under 13: see below). Google handles this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The game sends AdMob nothing else about you. For players under 13, every request is marked as a request for a child: Google does not use the advertising ID, and only ads rated for general audiences (G) are shown (see Children). This version of the game on iPhone has no reward videos.

Purchases (Google Play, and our check)

On Android, purchases are made through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details, name or email address; Google Play gives us an order number for each purchase. This version of the game on iPhone has no purchases.

So that each purchase is real and delivered once, the game sends our server the purchase's details from Google Play: the product bought, the game's package name and Google's purchase token (a code for this one purchase), together with the game's anonymous sign-in described below. The server asks Google Play to confirm the purchase and keeps a record of it: the anonymous sign-in's random id, the product, Google's order number, the time of purchase and whether it was a test purchase, filed under a one-way code made from the purchase token. It does not keep the token itself.

Refunds: once a day our server reads Google Play's list of refunded or cancelled purchases and records, for each one it had delivered, the same random id, the product, Google's order number and when and why it was cancelled. When the game starts it asks the server whether any of its own purchases was refunded, sending only the one-way codes of the purchases in its save, and removes what a refunded purchase gave.

The server runs on Google Cloud (Cloud Run functions and Cloud Firestore, in the United States) for us. These purchase and refund records are kept for 3 years from when they are made, to stop a purchase being used twice, to handle refunds and to meet tax and accounting obligations, and are then deleted automatically.

The anonymous sign-in (Firebase Authentication)

To check purchases and to run INVITE FRIENDS, the game signs this installation in to Firebase Authentication, a service of Google, anonymously: Firebase creates a random id for the installation and nothing else is given to it - no name, email, phone number or account. The id stays on this phone; a new phone or a reinstall gets a new one. This sign-in is separate from the statistics question, because purchases and invites cannot work without it. On Android, these requests also carry a Firebase App Check token: Google Play Integrity, on your phone, tells Google that the request comes from this game as installed from Google Play on a genuine device, and the token carries only that answer - no statistics, usage data or personal details.

Invite friends

Each player has a six-letter invite code. When you share it, or enter a friend's code, the game stores in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud, for us): the code, the anonymous sign-in id of its owner, the anonymous ids of the players who joined with it, and which of them have won stage 10 and stage 40, so both sides can be rewarded. The game does not show this to other players, but a record is read by its code: anyone who knows a code can read that code's record (the random ids in it, nothing else). On Android, if you install the game from a friend's invite link, the game reads the link's code from Google Play's install referrer on your phone; only the code is kept. INVITE FRIENDS is not offered to players under 13.

Writing to us, and test builds

CONTACT US in Settings opens your own mail app with a message that includes the game's version, your phone's model and system, and your Player ID (a random code made by the game, not tied to your phone or any account). We receive it only if you send it, together with your email address. Support emails are deleted when the issue is closed, or on request. Test builds that we give to testers outside the stores can also share a play log of your attempts, only when you press SEND TEST LOG and choose where to send it; the versions on Google Play and the App Store do not have it.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone - full hearts, the daily gift, the end of a pass season, and a few after you have been away; on Android also the daily challenge and the end of a league week. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us to create them, and they never contain offers. You can turn all of them, or each kind, off in the game's settings or your phone's settings.

Checking the time

To stop timed rewards from being collected early by changing the phone's clock, the game asks a web server for the current time: www.apple.com on iPhone, www.google.com on Android. The request carries nothing about you or your game. As with any internet request, the server sees your device's IP address, under Apple's or Google's own privacy policy. The game does not record or keep it.

Checking for a new version

The game reads a small public file on this website, version.json, which says which version of the game is the newest on each store, so it can tell you when an update is ready. The same file can carry a short notice from us and can switch a feature off for everyone if it breaks. When that file says there is new MAIL, the game also reads mail.json, a public file with the studio's messages and gifts; a message meant for one player names them only by a one-way code made from their Player ID, which the game checks on the phone. Gifts from inviting friends, leagues and coming back are worked out by the game on the phone and shown in MAIL. These requests carry nothing about you or your game. As with any internet request, the website's host (GitHub) sees your device's IP address, under GitHub's own privacy policy. The game does not record or keep it.

Team mode and Quick Match (Photon)

If you choose to play TEAM mode, the game connects to Photon, a multiplayer service run by Exit Games GmbH (Germany), to link your phone with your friends' phones. While you are in a room, Photon receives your device's IP address and the game data needed to play together: the room code, your player name, your hero, its level and your weapon, where you move and shoot, your health and your kills. The other players in the room see your player name, your hero and how it plays. In a TEAM room (joined with a room code), the other players see your Google Play Games name (Android) or Game Center name (iPhone) if you are signed in to it; otherwise they see a gamer tag the game makes for you. In Quick Match, other players see only a gamer tag the game makes for you. A player under 13 (by the age screen or the store) always gets a made-up name, a new one for every match, and never an account name. The game sends Photon no email or advertising ID. We use Photon only to connect the players; Photon handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://www.photonengine.com/privacy. Playing alone does not connect to Photon. Game traffic in TEAM and Quick Match is not encrypted.

Quick Match puts you in a team with other players without a room code. The game measures which Photon region answers fastest and searches there. To make teams of similar strength, it sends Photon your team size and a strength level worked out from your hero's level and your weapon's tier. In Quick Match every player, you included, is shown only by a name the game makes, never by a Google Play Games or Game Center name. In TEAM and Quick Match runs, players can talk only with a fixed set of short phrases and markers built into the game, with no free text, and can give each other likes, which the game counts. Photon passes these on to the players in the run and the game does not keep them on any server. So that you can come back to a run after a short drop in connection, the game sends the room a random key that it makes new each time the app starts. The key identifies no one and is not kept after the app closes. As our Terms of Service say (section 7), in Quick Match computer-controlled teammates may fill empty team seats, and in TEAM and Quick Match a computer-controlled teammate may carry on the hero of a player who leaves the run, loses connection or is away.

Where your progress is stored

Your progress, coins, purchased items and settings are saved on your device, protected against editing. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them from the device.

On Android, the game offers to sign in to Google Play Games when it starts. Signing in is optional: if you decline, you can connect later from CLOUD SAVE in the game's Settings. If you sign in, a copy of your progress (stages, coins, diamonds, heroes and weapons, the record of purchases delivered, and which MAIL you have opened, claimed or deleted, protected against editing, without your phone's details) is saved to your Google Play Games account through Google's Saved Games service, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Google keeps that copy under its own privacy policy; we cannot see it. Your game settings on the phone, such as your answer to the statistics question, are not included. You can delete the copy and stop the backup in the Google Play Games app (your profile, then settings) or at myaccount.google.com. On iPhone there is no backup by the game.

The app stores

Google Play and the App Store handle the download, and Google Play handles payments, and they may collect information about them under their own privacy policies. Apart from the purchase details described above, we receive only the aggregated, anonymous statistics the stores give every developer (for example, how many people installed the game) and the sales reports they give every seller.

Your age

The game asks your age once, on a neutral screen. Your answer stays only on your device: it is never sent to us, to our service providers or to anyone else, and it is not part of your cloud save. The game uses it on your device only, to limit features: if you are under 13, it sends no statistics, on Android shows only reward videos that are non-personalised and suitable for children, and offers no friend invites; if you are under 18, it turns off advertising measurement. If your app store tells the game you are younger, the younger age applies.

Children

The game is made for a general audience of teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families programme; players under 18 may play only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian (see the Terms of Service). If a player's answer on the age screen, or the app store, tells the game that a player is under 13, the game sends no statistics and no crash reports; on Android offers only reward videos that are non-personalised and suitable for children; offers no INVITE FRIENDS; sets no reminders; and does not ask for a store rating.

Such a player can still play TEAM and Quick Match. There, Photon receives their device's IP address and the game data described under "Team mode and Quick Match" only to connect and run the match; we keep none of it. They can use only the fixed phrases, markers and likes built into the game, never free text, and they are shown only by a name the game makes, a new random one for every match, in Quick Match and in TEAM alike. On Android, the anonymous sign-in and App Check described above still work for them, only so that a purchase can be checked. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, write to us and we will delete it.

When the age screen or the app store tells the game that a player is under 13, the game stops statistics at once and deletes the statistics still waiting on the phone to be sent, both the game's own and those Firebase was holding; nothing more is sent. Statistics sent before then are deleted on request (see "Your rights") or at the end of the periods above. From then on, apart from purchase records (see "Purchases"), we collect nothing new about such a player.

For such a player, the identifiers the game still uses (the anonymous sign-in and App Check token, and the connection to Photon) serve only to run the game: to check a purchase, to protect it against fraud, and to connect and run TEAM and Quick Match. We do not use them to contact the player, to build a profile of them or for advertising, and we give them to no one for those purposes: they are never linked to the Player ID, to statistics or to the advertising ID, and the only records we keep of them are the purchase records described above.

Players under 13. When the age screen or the app store tells the game that a player is under 13, the game collects no personal information from that player except the persistent identifiers and technical data described here and above, used only to support the internal operations of the game. It sends no statistics and no crash reports, and the game attaches no Player ID, name or advertising ID to any request. On Android, the game still shows that player optional reward videos through Google AdMob. Every video request for that player is marked as a request for a child (Google's child-directed treatment): Google turns off personalised and interest-based ads and remarketing, does not use the device's advertising ID, disables third-party ad measurement, and shows only ads rated suitable for general audiences (G). To deliver such a video, AdMob necessarily receives the device's IP address and basic technical information (device model, operating system, app version, language). Under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, we and Google use these only to support the internal operations of the game: to serve contextual ads (chosen from this app and the moment, not from the player); to limit how often an ad is shown (frequency capping); to detect fraud and invalid traffic and keep the service secure; and to keep the ad service working and measure that it works. They are never used to build a profile of the player, to contact the player, or for behavioural or targeted advertising, and the game itself never stores them. Google acts as our service provider for this purpose.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to see the information we hold about your installation, and ask us to correct it if it is wrong, incomplete or out of date (sections 13 and 14 of Israel's Privacy Protection Law), or to delete it: use CONTACT US in the game's Settings, or write to the address below, with the Player ID shown in the game's Settings. We answer within 30 days, and we complete a deletion within 30 days of your request, except for the records described below that we must keep; Google then removes its backup copies on its own schedule.

To ask us to delete the statistics kept for your installation, you can also tap DELETE DATA in the game's Settings, which writes the request for you. Basic counts (sent on Android whatever you answer) carry no Player ID, so they cannot be singled out for deletion; they are removed with the retention periods above. Invite records can be deleted on request too: include your invite code (YOUR CODE on the INVITE FRIENDS page), because invite records are filed by code, not by Player ID. Purchase and refund records are kept for 3 years to prevent fraud, handle refunds and meet legal obligations, as described above, and are then deleted automatically. If you have any other question about your privacy, write to us too.

Privacy laws in US states

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising: every reward video is requested as non-personalised. If you are 18 or over and agree to statistics, the advertising ID and the game events described above are shared with Google Ads only to measure our advertising campaigns (never to choose the ads you see); you can stop this at any time by switching DETAILED STATISTICS off in Settings. On Android, answering NO THANKS, or switching DETAILED STATISTICS off, is your opt-out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising, as US state privacy laws use those words. If you live in a US state with a privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah or Texas), you can ask us what we hold about your installation, and ask us to correct or delete it: write to the address below with the Player ID shown in the game's Settings. We answer within 30 days. If we turn down a request, you can ask us to look at it again by replying to our answer.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it does with information, for example by adding a new service or new statistics, this policy is updated before that version is released, and the date above changes.
